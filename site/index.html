01 / medium

Medium — Antenatal Wellness Toolkit

A haptic wellness companion and NHS-integrated service, designed to support antenatal emotional wellbeing at home. MRes thesis, 2026.

Daily voice notes, AI-summarised into a secure NHS clinician dashboard, close the gap between antenatal appointments and daily life at home — closing a care gap that leaves 1 in 5 new and expectant mothers to manage difficult emotions largely alone.

02 / ventrimon

VentriMon — Non-Invasive ICP Monitoring

Digital Medical Innovation Programme · 4 weeks · internship group project.

A gecko-adhesive inspired headband concept for continuous, non-invasive intracranial pressure monitoring — built with two engineers and a medical student to bring infection rates as high as 27% down to zero.
