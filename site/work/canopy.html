Canopy — a new-age insurance app for the youth

Making Life, Health, and Motor insurance approachable, so young adults can explore cover with confidence.

Role
Product Designer
Project Type
Academic Group Project
Duration
15 Weeks
Category
Finance Application

The Brief

Insurance, made for a younger audience

The insurance industry provides cover for life’s risks, letting people go about their day with a sense of assurance. Canopy asked: how can Life, Health, and Motor insurance appeal to young adults who don’t yet see the relevance?

Educate young people on insurance
Design an app that appeals to a younger audience
Encourage one app for all policies
Incorporate tips, tricks, discounts & offers
Make claiming a policy easier, online

Building an Identity

Mission, vision, values

Mission: Creating a new-age insurance platform which is user-friendly. Vision: Young adults actively involved and interested in purchasing insurance.

Transparency Simplicity Fun Education

Key Screens

Four flows, one app

Ten quick questions to understand a user’s needs and help experts provide tailor-made plans and policies.

In-app alerts and calendars remind users of upcoming or due premium payments.

Digitisation moves claiming a policy away from long queues and physical forms.

A separate, easy-to-navigate flow for beneficiaries in case of the insured person’s absence.
